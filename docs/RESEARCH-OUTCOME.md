# Research outcome

The final preregistered value audit recommended **NO-GO for continued standalone research development**. The tested graph construction did not demonstrate a practical advantage over standard retrieval methods or a small script over the raw source.

Three claims failed their predefined practical thresholds: metadata improved completion too little, the construction did not save effort on the selected evidence tasks, and a genre relation feature reduced retrieval performance. A fourth completion claim was inconclusive overall: the two main strata failed the threshold and the third was undersized. These outcomes do not prove no possible application exists.

The useful retained deliverable is an experimental local interface and reproducible engineering starting point. Query correctness is distinct from linguistic correctness. The experiments used edited, reading-derived signs, not photographs or independent natural-damage restoration targets. No restoration accuracy or novel decipherment is claimed.

The full protocols, results, original git histories and reviews are retained in the maintainer's private archive. They are not bundled with this public UI. The sign/group interpretation experiments in [FUTURE-EXPERIMENTS.md](FUTURE-EXPERIMENTS.md) were not tested by the audit.
